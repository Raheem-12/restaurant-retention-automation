HOW IT WORKS

A simple retention system built around the customer journey.

01

Customers join

Guests join a restaurant's customer list through a simple signup experience and choose whether to receive marketing.

02

Offers are delivered

DineSurge can automatically send unique offers and customer communications through connected restaurant accounts.

03

Redemptions are tracked

Restaurants can record offer redemptions and view customer engagement data without managing the workflow manually.

FOR RESTAURANTS

Designed to handle the operational work behind retention.

Automated customer offers

Deliver unique offers automatically after an eligible customer signs up.

Duplicate protection

Customer information can be checked before issuing a new offer to reduce repeated claims.

Redemption tracking

Track when offers are redeemed so restaurant teams can understand how the program is being used.

Restaurant-controlled data

DineSurge can connect to authorized restaurant accounts and customer-management tools used to operate the service.
