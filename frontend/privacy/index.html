Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

DineSurge provides customer retention and engagement tools for restaurants. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and processed when restaurants and their customers interact with DineSurge services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how a restaurant uses DineSurge, information processed through the service may include:

  • Customer names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Marketing consent selections
  • Signup dates and timestamps
  • Coupon or promotional offer information
  • Offer redemption status and timestamps
  • Restaurant account and configuration information

The specific information collected may vary depending on the restaurant and the features it chooses to use.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed through DineSurge may be used to operate restaurant customer-retention programs, including to:

  • Record customer signups
  • Determine eligibility for promotional offers
  • Detect duplicate offer claims
  • Generate and deliver customer offers
  • Record offer redemptions
  • Provide restaurant engagement and program information
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the service

3. Restaurant Customer Data

Restaurants using DineSurge may collect information directly from their customers. DineSurge processes this information as necessary to provide the customer-retention services configured for that restaurant.

Restaurants are responsible for determining the promotions they offer and for ensuring that their collection and use of customer information complies with applicable requirements.

4. Google Account Data

A restaurant or authorized user may choose to connect a Google account to DineSurge. DineSurge requests access only to Google services needed to provide the enabled functionality.

DineSurge may request permission to send email through the connected Google account. This access is used to send transactional or restaurant-authorized customer communications, such as customer offers.

DineSurge may also request permission to access Google Sheets. This access is used to read and update restaurant customer-retention records, including customer signups, duplicate checks, promotional offer information, redemption status, and related program data.

DineSurge does not request broader Gmail access for reading a user's mailbox when that access is not required to provide the service.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

DineSurge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Sharing of Information

DineSurge does not sell Google user data or restaurant customer information.

Information may be processed by service providers and infrastructure platforms when necessary to operate DineSurge, provide requested functionality, maintain security, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Data Security

DineSurge uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information processed through the service. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the applicable service, support restaurant operations, meet legitimate operational requirements, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

9. Marketing Communications

Marketing communications are intended to be sent only when authorized by the applicable restaurant and consistent with the customer's applicable consent or communication preferences.

Customers may request to stop receiving promotional communications using the unsubscribe method provided with the applicable communication or by contacting the restaurant or DineSurge where appropriate.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as DineSurge develops or its services change. The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was most recently updated.
