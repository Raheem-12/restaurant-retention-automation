Privacy Policy

This demonstration system collects information that users voluntarily provide, such as name, email address, phone number, and marketing consent status.

This information is used to operate the customer retention demonstration, including issuing offers, preventing duplicate offer claims, tracking offer redemption, and communicating with users who have provided consent.

Personal information is not sold to third parties.

Information may be processed using service providers required to operate the system, such as hosting, email, automation, and data-storage providers.

Users may request that their information be corrected or deleted, or may withdraw marketing consent, by contacting the operator of this demonstration.
